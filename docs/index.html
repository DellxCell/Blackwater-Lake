At the lake

A whole Survival map around the lake cabin

  • Pack-a-PunchOut on the snowy deck of the cabin.
  • Der WunderfizzInside the cabin.
  • Rampage InducerOn the barrel by the cabin wall. Turn it on when you want the rounds to speed up.
  • T.E.D.D. TasksIn Cursed Survival, from the console at the cabin; the reward box is in the main room.

Modes

Pick it like any other map

  • SurvivalZombies > Survival > Blackwater Lake.
  • Cursed SurvivalZombies > Cursed Survival > Blackwater Lake, relics included.
  • SquadsSolo, or a squad of up to 4.
  • ExfilAfter a successful exfil the match ends and moves on by itself.

Also

Kronos features that work at the lake

  • Mister Peeks MayhemPick the Mr. Peeks relic in Cursed Survival; shoot him above the cabin entrance to drop an egg.
  • Restore PowerThe objective shows on the HUD, with its timer during the pump and vines step.
  • KlausSummon Klaus and he fights alongside you.
  • Loading introA 15-second picture intro of the cabin, pool room, living room, pyramid and entrance.

Get started

  1. Set up Kronos and run its launcher at least once.
  2. Download Blackwater-Lake-1.0.zip from the latest release.
  3. Close Black Ops 7 and KronosLauncher, then extract the zip into C:\Kronos\cod25\kronos\mods. You should end up with mods\blackwater_lake\mod.json.
  4. Launch through Kronos and pick Blackwater Lake in Survival or Cursed Survival.

Good to know

  • Had the old version you copied into the Kronos folder (November 8)? Press Update Now in the Kronos launcher and delete the old Blackwater files first; the README lists them.
  • Avoid Save & Quit on this map; a Blackwater save may resume as Ashes of the Damned.
  • Squad play hasn't been fully tested yet.
